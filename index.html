Management Policy

PT. Java Agritech was established in 1994 and celebrated its 30th anniversary this year.

It is thanks to all of Customers, Shareholders, and Employees that we were able to expand our business from starting with Wasabi to Spices Vegetables fruit and curry.

With collaboration between Japanese and Indonesian, our company will continue to work hard and improve to provide the world with safe, secure and high quality products based on Japanese standards. 

Thank you.

Mr. Tomohito Matsui
President Director

"Java Agritech: Good Agriculture Practice"

Java Agritech is a food industry company dedicated to fostering a sustainable future by producing its own agricultural products through innovative farming and global good agricultural practice standards (Global.G.A.P certified for wasabi farms), with Agritechnology solutions.

Expert

  • Diverse climate and soil condition (lowland to highland)
    Over 25 years experience and expertise in farm management.
  • A series of cultivation activities from compost production to cultivation/harvesting with own farming team.
  • Strict pesticide management.

Diverse Crops

  • Diverse crops support a variety of beneficial organisms such as pollinators, natural predators, and soil microbes, fostering a healthier ecosystem.
  • Biodiversity in agriculture contributes to the conservation of plant genetic resources, preserving valuable traits for future crop development.

Production System

  • Global standard quality control system (HACCP/ISO9001:2015).
  • Product development for customer’s requirements
    Flexible production line that enables production in small lot.

Safe, Value, Comfortable

Food safety and safe workplace creation, Continuous improvement to maximize created value, and Creating a workplace where employees can understand their duties and carry out them comfortably.
